You Have To Start Selling Before You're Ready

Start With Customers, Not Products

Business success starts by finding people with problems, not building products first. Most entrepreneurs make the mistake of creating websites, brands, and social media before validating demand. The correct approach flips the process: get customers to start your business.

  • Ask people you know if they have problems needing solutions. For example, ask: 'Do you know anyone needing AI marketing automation?'
  • Don't ask if they want your service. Instead, ask if they know someone who does. This avoids rejection and builds referrals.
  • Sarah contacted her phone contacts after 9 months of hesitation. She made thousands of dollars within weeks of implementing the method.
